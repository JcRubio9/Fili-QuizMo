About the Game

Interactive Filipino learning practice with progress students can see.

Gameplay

Filipino Quizzes

Students complete themed quiz challenges in Filipino grammar, literature, and reading comprehension to earn points.

Progress

Badges

🏅 🏆 ⭐ 🎖️ 🥇

Each completed quiz level awards a special badge, giving learners a clear sense of accomplishment as they advance.

Learning

DepEd-Aligned

About School

Little Einstein Discovery School of Caloocan

Little Einstein Discovery School of Caloocan is an educational institution established in 2011 that provides a holistic, interactive, and innovative learning experience for young learners.

Holistic Development

Paglinang sa kakayahan sa pag-iisip, pakikisalamuha, emosyon, at pisikal na aspekto ng bawat mag-aaral.

Supportive Environment

Pagbibigay ng makabuluhang aktibidad at ligtas na kapaligiran para sa epektibong pagkatuto mula noong 2011.
